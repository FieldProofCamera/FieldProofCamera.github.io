FieldProof Camera stamps every photo and video with the real date and time, GPS location, address and your own notes, and seals the picture so nobody can quietly change the time, place or details afterwards. It is built for work where a photo is evidence.

How the proof works

Trusted time

The time is checked against internet time servers or GPS, not the phone clock, so changing the phone's date does not change the stamp.

GPS and address

Coordinates, accuracy, address, compass, altitude and speed are printed on the photo and saved inside the file. Fake-GPS apps are detected and marked.

Tamper seal

Every photo is signed with a key created inside the phone that never leaves it. Changing even one digit, such as an odometer reading, makes the photo show as EDITED when it is checked.

Security QR strip

A signed QR code under the photo carries the photo ID, time and place. It survives WhatsApp compression, so a forwarded photo can still be checked.

Sealed ZIP sharing

Photos can be sent as a ZIP, which messaging apps do not compress, so the full seal arrives intact.

Built-in verification

Anyone with the app can open a photo or ZIP and see ORIGINAL, EDITED or NOT VERIFIED, plus the location on Google Maps.

Privacy policy

FieldProof Camera is made by ASIF KHALID. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Everything stays on your phone

FieldProof Camera has no account, no server of its own, no ads and no analytics. Photos, videos, locations and settings are saved only on your phone, in the folder you choose. Nothing is uploaded by the app.

Camera and microphone

Used only to take the photos and videos you ask for. Sound is recorded only while you record a video.

Location

Used only while the camera screen is open, to print GPS coordinates on your photos and store them in the file. The address is looked up with Android's built-in geocoder, which sends the coordinates to your phone's location provider (usually Google).

Trusted time

To stop fake times, the app asks public time servers (time.google.com, time.cloudflare.com, pool.ntp.org) for the current time. Only a standard time request is sent; no personal data.

Security seal

Each photo is signed with a key that is created inside your phone and never leaves it. The photo ID, time and location are stored inside the photo and in its QR code so that anyone you send the photo to can check it.

Sharing

Photos leave your phone only when you share them yourself (WhatsApp, email and so on). Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Children

FieldProof Camera is a work tool and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Delete photos in the app's gallery or your phone's gallery. Uninstalling the app removes its settings and its signing key.
